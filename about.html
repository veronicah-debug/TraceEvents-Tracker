About Our System

Trace Events Tracker was developed as part of the DBT 1305 Web Application Development team project. Our goal is to streamline campus event management and eliminate manual sign-ups.

Core Objectives

Provide secure user authentication for students, organizers, and admins; maintain automated attendance records; and offer transparent financial budget estimations for campus events. To identify the challenges faced by students and organizers in managing events, and to provide a user-friendly platform that simplifies event registration, ticket tracking, and budget management.

Our system is designed to enhance the overall event experience for students and organizers alike, ensuring that events are well-organized, accessible, and enjoyable for all participants.
